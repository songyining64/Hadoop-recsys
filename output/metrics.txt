=== Protocol ===
Train/test: TIME — last event per user is test (tie-break by item_id); profiles from strictly prior train history only.
Features (classifiers): train-only user_profile + item_profile aggregates only; no row-level like_num/rating, interaction_rate, purchase_intent, or MR implicit.
Default RECSYS_EVAL: time_last if CSV has event_ts, else loo_pos. Override: time_last | loo_pos | user_random.
Train internal: 85/15 train/val (seed=11) on train split rows for model & α.
Negative sampling: train negatives downsampled toward ~1:3 pos:neg on train split.
Item-CF: user-level co-occurrence with category proxy item (see report).
Fusion: best model among LR/GBT/RF by val AUC; α chosen by val AUC on labeled candidates.
